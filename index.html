📌 Outreach Tips for Google Voice
1
Always identify yourself immediately. Say "Hey, this is [Name] from Haulxify — I'm calling about your trucking operation." Operators hang up on anonymous callers in seconds.
2
Best call windows: 7–9 AM and 4–6 PM local time. Operators are off the road and more likely to pick up. Avoid midday — they're often driving or loading.
3
No answer? Leave a voicemail and follow up with Email 1. The combo of a voicemail + personalized email within the same hour dramatically increases response rates.
4
Keep calls under 3 minutes on first contact. The goal is a callback or a "tell me more" — not closing on call one. Ask one question: "Are you currently using a dispatcher?"
5
Enable voicemail transcription in Settings. Google Voice will auto-transcribe every voicemail so you can read them instantly without listening — huge time saver during busy shifts.
